# Hi, I'm Vitor 👋

Backend developer focused on **.NET** and **C#**, building APIs, async processing and message-broker integrations — with a particular interest in **Elixir** and **Go**. I like keeping a critical eye on architecture, always aiming for the most viable solution for the moment together with the team — and building products from the ground up.

- 🔎 I enjoy venturing into new technologies, exploring what's out there and keeping my creativity active beyond code.
- 🎮 In my spare time I also enjoy making games — check some of it out on my [itch.io](https://vblo.itch.io/).
- 🎵 I also try my hand at making music — you can find it on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/vitor-rabelo-5) and [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/intl-pt/artist/3nqNvEawc1xTYyE6hDXWPD?si=DdwH7prnRi-6jlHa5Az2PQ).
